Privacy Policy — YTAIOS

Last updated: 2026-09-21

  • Scope: YTAIOS is a personal-use tool built and operated by Praneel Girish. It is not distributed to the public and has exactly one user.
  • Data accessed: Through Google OAuth, it accesses only the YouTube channel, Google Sheets files, and Google Docs files that the developer's own Google account already owns or has been granted access to.
  • What it does with that data: Data is read or written only to perform actions the developer explicitly runs. Nothing is analyzed, sold, or repurposed beyond that action.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens are stored locally on the developer's own machine. No data is sent to or stored on any third-party server.
  • Sharing: No data is shared with any other person, service, or company.
  • Retention & deletion: Deleting the local token file immediately revokes local access; access can also be revoked anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: girishpraneel@gmail.com
