single-operator tool that connects Praneel Girish's own Google account to his own YouTube channel, Google Sheets, and Google Docs, so his content pipeline can run from the terminal instead of by hand. What it does Uploads and manages videos, playlists, and comments on one YouTube channel the developer owns; reads and writes a small number of Google Sheets and Docs used for scripting and planning. Users One — the developer, on his own accounts. Distribution Not published or shared. Runs locally.
